About the MIRRAGE Collective

Advancing defensive capabilities and open-source threat intelligence.

Our Organization

Founded in 2021 by a distributed group of systems engineers and vulnerability researchers, MIRRAGE provides an independent platform for sharing threat intelligence, defensive configurations, and security research.

We believe that open analysis and decentralized peer review are the most effective means to combat sophisticated cyber threats. Our community operates across public research tracks and private working groups.

Our Mission

Our core mission is to empower defensive engineers with actionable, peer-reviewed telemetry and robust validation methodologies. We work to:

  • Analyze emerging attack surfaces in critical infrastructure and open software stacks.
  • Develop lightweight diagnostic tools and automated syntax verification utilities.
  • Provide reproducible benchmarks for defensive security implementations.
