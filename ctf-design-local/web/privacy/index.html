Privacy Policy

Transparency regarding data collection, logging, and user submission telemetry.

1. Information We Collect

The MIRRAGE platform prioritizes privacy and minimal data retention. When you access our services, our systems automatically collect:

  • Server Access Logs: Standard HTTP request parameters including IP address, request URI, HTTP status code, user agent, and timestamp. Logs are rotated every 14 days.
  • Forum Submissions & Diagnostics: Thread contents, submission handles, and preprocessor execution output generated during validation runs.
  • Session State: Transient session cookies used solely for navigation and authentication state.

2. How We Use Data

Data collected via the platform is utilized exclusively for:

  • Maintaining operational stability and detecting malicious network activity.
  • Verifying thread submission integrity and executing requested content preprocessors.
  • Preventing automated spam and denial-of-service attempts against community nodes.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with external third parties or advertisers.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or marketing beacons. Only strictly necessary first-party functional cookies are stored to maintain application state.

4. Contact & Data Subject Requests

For inquiries regarding telemetry data, log retention, or data deletion requests, contact our privacy officer at:
