Security Architecture & Policies

Our operational standards for system integrity, vulnerability triage, and disclosure.

1. Defensive Practices

The MIRRAGE platform implements defense-in-depth principles across all public-facing services. Core tenets include:

  • Principle of Least Privilege: Web endpoints and auxiliary processors run under restricted unprivileged service accounts (such as www-data) to prevent direct system compromise.
  • Isolation Boundaries: Internal validation scripts and experimental preprocessor routines are segregated from administrative nodes.
  • Continuous Log Auditing: Endpoint transactions and formatting pipelines are monitored for anomalous syntax execution.

2. Responsible Disclosure Policy

We encourage ethical researchers and community members to report potential security vulnerabilities responsibly. We adhere to a 90-day coordinated disclosure policy.

When reporting issues:

  • Provide detailed reproduction steps and an explanation of potential impact.
  • Avoid accessing or modifying data that does not belong to your test account.
  • Allow our infrastructure team reasonable time to triage and remediate issues before public disclosure.

3. Account & Credential Hygiene

Platform operators and contributors must adhere to strict credential requirements. Passwords must be at least 14 characters in length and stored using modern salted hashing algorithms. Remote administrative access is limited to key-authenticated SSH tunnels on non-standard ports.
